Android ou le constructeur peut néanmoins sauvegarder le répertoire privé selon vos réglages système. Ce processus est géré par le système ; VisdAR n'y a aucun accès ni contrôle.

The Android manifest declares no permissions, including no INTERNET, location, contacts, camera or microphone permission. The app therefore cannot itself transmit data off the device.

Local operation

3. Local operation, storage and external links

  • The interface, colour data, font and game logic are packaged as static resources inside a local WebView.
  • No server, database, cloud sync, cookie or persistent user profile is used.
  • When you deliberately tap a reference link, it opens in the phone's external browser. The destination may process browser data under its own policy; VisdAR does not receive it.

Android Auto Backup

The app declares allowBackup=true. It stores no personal data, so there is normally no personal content to back up. Android or the phone manufacturer may nevertheless back up the app-private area according to your system settings. The operating system manages this process; VisdAR cannot access or control it.

Third parties and children

4. Third-party services and children's privacy

No advertising, analytics, crash reporting, payment, login or social SDK is included. The font and colour references are embedded static resources and do not contact their authors or sources.

The app is suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected, no children's personal data is collected, processed or shared.

5. Your rights, retention and security
