The application includes an adapted, compact index that retains entries only
when a Chinese written form and its complete pinyin tone sequence match a
CFDICT example. Cross-reference-only and unqualified religious glosses are
excluded. This adapted English index is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. The
French CFDICT data and English CC-CEDICT data remain separate sources.
