3. 数据如何存储

  • 无任何云端服务器：没有后端、没有数据库、没有云同步。
  • 本应用为打包在 APK 内的本地 WebView，农历、节气、干支、节假日算法与数据全部静态嵌入。

关于 Android 自动备份（allowBackup=true）

AndroidManifest.xml 中声明了 android:allowBackup="true"（Google 推荐默认值），含义是：

  • 如果您在系统设置中开启了「Google 备份」或手机厂商的云端备份（如小米云、华为云、三星云等），则应用的私有设置（例如主题偏好）可能由 Android 系统自动上传至您本人账户下的备份空间。
  • 该过程完全由 Android 操作系统执行，开发者（VisdAR）无法读取、无法访问、也无法控制这些备份。
  • 如不希望备份：在系统设置 系统 → 备份 中关闭，或通过 adb 单独对本应用关闭。

Storage

3. Where & how data is stored

  • No servers, no databases, no cloud sync are used.
  • The app is a local WebView shipped inside the APK. Lunar algorithms, solar terms, holidays and the full calendar engine are statically embedded.
  • Theme preferences are kept in the app-private directory (SharedPreferences / LocalStorage), inaccessible to other apps.

Android Auto-backup (allowBackup=true)

The manifest declares android:allowBackup="true" (Google's default recommended value). This means :

  • If you have enabled Google Backup in your system settings (or an OEM cloud backup such as Xiaomi Cloud, Huawei Cloud, Samsung Cloud…), the app's private preferences may be automatically uploaded by Android into the backup space tied to your own personal account.
  • This process is 100% handled by the Android OS. VisdAR has no access, no visibility and no control over those backups.
  • To turn it off : Settings → System → Backup, or use adb to disable backup for this specific app.
